Vocabdeck Cards - German word cards (Android, installable web app)

HOW TO GET IT ON YOUR PHONE
1. Put this whole folder online (it must be https). Easiest: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag this folder onto the page.
   Netlify gives you a link like https://something.netlify.app
2. Open that link in Chrome on your Android phone.
3. Chrome menu (three dots) > "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
4. Open Vocabdeck from your home screen, tap "Download words" once. After that it works offline.

NOTES
- Progress is saved on the phone. Use Settings > Export backup now and then.
- For the voice button, a German voice must be installed: Android Settings > Text-to-speech > Google > Install voice data > German.
- The Supabase URL and the public read-only key are in config.js.
